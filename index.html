ROTATE24 localized damped complex receptive fields. MASS changes their local rotation/decay time, not their coupling amplitude.
MASSSlow non-negative residue written by activation. It relaxes in global time while slowing local resonator time.
LATCHPersistent signed configuration written by strong coherent local input. It changes later phase/routing.
ROUTETwo competing outputs per channel share a fixed budget. Strengthening one weakens the other.

The MATERIAL EYE is a readout of the processor, not a claim that the system stores a face or reconstructs pixels. The 24 channels are six coarse image regions × four orientations, each with complex fast state plus slower material state. On gray, any structure you see on the right is internally generated by state that has not relaxed. The fixed probe remains the cleaner measurement.
